Project Overview
This website was developed as part of Web development. 
CueMotion is an online store that sells fitness equipment, 
gym wear, and accessories. The goal is to provide customers 
with an easy way to browse products and make enquiries online.

Website Goals and Objectives
1. To showcase CueMotion products to potential customers
2. To provide company information and build trust
3. To allow customers to send enquiries through a contact form
4. To display customer reviews and testimonials

Key Features and Functionality
1. Home page with navigation to all sections
2. About page with company details
3. Products page listing items for sale
4. Enquiry page with contact form
5. Reviews page with customer feedback
6. Organized folder structure with css, js, and images folders
